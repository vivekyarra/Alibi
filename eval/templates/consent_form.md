## What we ask

We ask to record video in which you may appear. We use the videos to test and measure GRIDPRINT.

Tick each item you agree to:

- [ ] I agree to appear in test videos recorded by the GRIDPRINT team.
- [ ] I agree that these videos are stored for the evaluation and are seen by the team members who run the evaluation.
- [ ] I agree that edited versions of these videos are made for testing (cuts, insertions, recompression, platform uploads that are deleted after download).
- [ ] **Face composites (optional, team members only):** I agree that my face is used in face-swap composite videos made with an open-source tool for testing (see `eval/FACE_COMPOSITES.md`). I understand that these composites are labeled as synthetic and are not published outside the evaluation, except as stills or short excerpts in the competition report or video if I tick the next box.
- [ ] **Publication (optional):** I agree that short excerpts or stills in which I appear are shown in the competition video, technical report, or demo endpoint.

## What we do not do

- We do not use face recognition. GRIDPRINT does not identify people.
- We do not share the full videos outside the team, except where you ticked "Publication".
- We do not sell or license the videos.

## Your rights

- You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting the team contact above. We then delete the videos in which you appear from the dataset and do not use them in future results. Results that were already published cannot always be changed.
- You can ask which videos include you.

## Retention

We delete the videos when the evaluation work for the competition ends, unless you agree in writing to a longer period.
